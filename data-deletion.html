SipToFit User Data Deletion Instructions

App Name: SipToFit

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

How to Request Deletion of Your Data

SipToFit respects your privacy and provides you with the ability to request deletion of your personal data, including any data obtained through Facebook Login.

Steps to Delete Your Data

  1. Send an email to info@insisone.com
  2. Use the subject line: "SipToFit Data Deletion Request"
  3. Include the email address associated with your SipToFit / Facebook account

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your account information (name, email, profile data)
  • Hydration tracking history and logs
  • Any data received from Facebook Login

Response Time

We will process your data deletion request within 7 business days.

Contact

For any questions: info@insisone.com
